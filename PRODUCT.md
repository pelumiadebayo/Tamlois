# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Firebase modular SDK, React Hook Form, Zod, date-fns, Lucide, Vitest, React Testing Library, and Playwright. The build must deploy as a GitHub Pages compatible SPA using hash routing.

## Users

The primary users are African women and natural-hair clients who are concerned about their scalp or hair and may not know which professional service to choose. They need calm education, clear boundaries around clinical claims, transparent placeholder pricing, and a low-friction booking path.

The secondary user is the clinic owner, Popoola Adebola Opeyemi, who needs to manage services, availability, bookings, public content, and setup status without exposing customer records publicly.

## Product Purpose

Tamlois explains hair and scalp concerns in plain language, presents personalised evidence-informed care, and converts an uncertain visitor into an appropriate consultation or service booking. It also provides a demo-ready operational interface that can move to Firebase without rewriting the UI.

## Positioning

The product begins with the concern a client recognises, then connects education, professional assessment, service selection, and booking in one continuous path. It does not diagnose online or promise regrowth.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Instagram, search, or referrals; explore a concern or service; build trust through the care process and consent-aware placeholder results; then book an appointment. The clinic operates by appointment Monday to Saturday, 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Sunday is closed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public marketing, concern education, service and package catalogue, demo shop, booking, lead capture, contact, policies, and results routes.
- Demo mode uses typed repositories and local storage. Firebase mode uses Authentication, Firestore rules, and transaction-based deterministic slot locks.
- Mock payments, email, analytics, and commerce adapters are required. Real Paystack verification and email delivery require a secure backend.
- GitHub Pages hosting and hash routing are required.
- All invented prices, durations, clinical copy, testimonials, results, media, contact details, and policies are placeholders and must be easy to replace.
- The supplied logo image is absent from the available attachment set. A temporary text mark may be used, but the original identity must not be redrawn or implied to be processed.

## Brand Commitments

The formal name is “Tamlois Natural & More | Tamlois Trichology Clinic.” Standard interface naming is “Tamlois Naturals & Trichology Clinic,” and the compact name is “Tamlois.” The experience must be minimalist, natural, classy, professional, calm, credible, health-focused, and welcoming. DM Serif Display is reserved for editorial headings; Manrope is used for navigation, forms, body copy, and operations. Green is the primary brand family, pending extraction from the missing source logo.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied factual evidence is limited to the business names, clinic description, owner name and description, opening hours, service families, required feature set, and visual direction. No final logo file, portrait, clinic photography, testimonials, results, prices, address, contact details, policy language, Firebase project, Shopify store, Paystack account, email provider, or analytics property is available. Future work must not treat placeholders as verified claims.

## Product Principles

1. Start with the client’s concern, not industry terminology.
2. Make the next safe action obvious without diagnosing or promising outcomes.
3. Keep operational data behind repository and authorization boundaries.
4. Keep demo mode complete and candid about what is not connected.
5. Make every placeholder traceable and replaceable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, reduced-motion support, clear validation, accessible date and time controls, comfortable touch targets, and imagery that primarily represents Black women and textured natural hair.
